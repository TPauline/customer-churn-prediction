Customer Churn Prediction

A full-stack machine learning project that predicts which telecom customers are likely to cancel — before they do.

Python scikit-learn pandas SMOTE Streamlit Logistic Regression

What It Does

📊

Dataset Overview

Scores all 7,000+ customers by churn risk and surfaces the highest-risk accounts in a sortable table.

👤

Single Customer Prediction

Enter any customer's details and instantly get a churn probability score with a visual risk gauge.

🎚️

Adjustable Risk Threshold

A sidebar slider lets the business team set how aggressive retention campaigns should be.

📈

Risk Distribution Charts

Visualises churn risk scores across the full customer base and historical churn rates by contract type.
